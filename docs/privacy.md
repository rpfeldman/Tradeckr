# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 24th, 2026

by: Ramiro Pablo Feldman

We built **Tradeckr** as an **open-source subscription-based app**. This page explains what information **Tradeckr** handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using **Tradeckr**, you agree to this policy.

## The short version

Your financial data stays on your device. It only leaves it if you choose to send us a bug report, which you can review before sending. We don't have servers that store your data, we don't use analytics or advertising, and we don't sell or share anything.

## Information Collection and Use

### Data stored on your device
All the information you enter in **Tradeckr** is stored only on your device. We do not have servers that store it and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

### Data you choose to send us
If you decide to report a bug, **Tradeckr** prepares an email in your device's email app. You can review, edit or delete its content before sending it. It includes:
- Device information such as: operating system and its version, manufacturer, model, language, etc.

- Actions and events that took place during your usage sessions including registered transactions with its value, category, currency, etc, screens visited, settings changed, etc.

- Your email address, since the report is sent from your own email account.

We use this information only to diagnose and fix problems and to reply to you.

## Third-Party Services
**Tradeckr** uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:
- **Exchange API by fawazahmed0**: used to download exchange rates. The data is served through the **jsDelivr** CDN. No personal or financial data is sent, but jsDelivr can see your IP address. For more information visit: https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api and https://jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net
- **Google Play / Apple App Store**: used to distribute the app, and, if applicable: to process subscription payments. We never see your payment card details. For more information visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy or https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

## Data Retention and Deletion
Bug report emails are kept for as long as necessary to diagnose and resolve the issue. You may request their deletion at any time by writing to support@tradeckr.com.

## Legal Basis
We process bug report data based on your consent, which you give by choosing to send the report. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete it.

## Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@tradeckr.com. 

## Security
Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. Bug reports travel through your email provider, so their security in transit depends on that provider.

## Children's Privacy
**Tradeckr** is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@tradeckr.com.
